About

Polystellar

3D Artist & Generalist — 3 years in, self-taught

I make things that don't exist yet look like they always did — characters, spaces and objects built with the same care whether they're seen for two seconds in a game or two minutes in a film.

Three years moving between hard-surface product work, organic character sculpting and environment lighting — sharing the process on YouTube along the way. I like problems that require learning a new pipeline to solve them.

Tools

Blender Maya ZBrush Substance Painter Krita Unreal Engine

02

Process

Reference and blockout first, then sculpt or model to final topology, texture in Substance, and light every piece as if it were the only shot in the reel.
